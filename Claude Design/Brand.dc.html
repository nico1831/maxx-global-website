About the manufacturer

The only commercial cardio line that gives power back to your building.

SportsArt has built commercial fitness equipment since 1977, designing in Washington State and manufacturing in Tainan. The company's defining work is ECO-POWR: a self-generating drive system that converts up to 74% of a user's mechanical output into utility-grade electricity and returns it to the building's supply.

For a Philippine facility running long cardio hours — a hotel gym, a corporate floor, a 24-hour club — that changes the arithmetic of a purchase. Equipment normally sits on the cost side of a budget; ECO-POWR machines put a recurring number on the other side, and give a sustainability officer something concrete to report.

We have carried the line since 2012 and hold parts locally. Our technicians are factory-trained on the ECO-POWR drive, which matters: it is not a system a general service contractor should be opening.
