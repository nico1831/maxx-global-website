The brief

A staff fitness centre for roughly 1,200 mall and head-office employees, on a mezzanine level directly above retail trading floor. Three constraints shaped the specification: noise and vibration could not transfer downwards during trading hours, the room had to serve peak shift changes at 06:00 and 22:00, and the whole installation had to happen without closing any retail space.

What we supplied

Forty-two units across cardio, selectorized strength and free weights, on a 20 mm interlocking rubber floor with an acoustic underlay beneath the cardio zone. The cardio row is entirely SportsArt ECO-POWR, which was specified as much for the client's sustainability reporting as for the equipment itself — the row now returns a measurable contribution to the mezzanine's supply.

Selectorized stations are Impulse, chosen for parts availability and because the maintenance team can service them without a specialist call-out. Flooring is FitPlus, laid by our own crew.

The outcome

Delivered and commissioned in eleven days, all work overnight, with no retail closure. Vibration readings on the trading floor below came in under the acoustic consultant's threshold at every measured point. The facility has since been extended into an adjacent storage bay — the second phase is with us now.
