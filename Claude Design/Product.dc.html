The G690 is SportsArt's flagship ECO-POWR treadmill: a self-generating commercial deck that converts up to 74% of a user's mechanical output into utility-grade electricity and feeds it back to the building. For hotels and corporate facilities running long cardio hours, that turns a fixed operating cost into a measurable reduction — and gives sustainability officers a number they can report. Built on a commercial 22" × 62" running surface with SportsArt's shock-absorption system and a 20-year frame warranty.
